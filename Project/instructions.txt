Instructions

1. You will navigate the world and areas by using the commands "left" or "right", actions will be completed by using "y" or "n"
2. Some of your actions will have consequences, some leading to more "catastrophic" consequences than others.
3. You can view your inventory at any time using "i".
4. Try to balance your resources, just in case you need them in the future...
5. Don't take the wrong path, it might not be rewarding, depending on the choices you make.
